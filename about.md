I'm Kyle Parrella, a senior at Loyola University Chicago completing a B.S. in Mathematics, a B.S. in Statistics and a B.A. in Economics (expected May 2027), with a 3.97 GPA and Dean's List every semester. I'm applying to law school.

Recently I submitted my working paper on putting honest uncertainty around machine-learning forecasts of stock-market volatility, which I wrote as first author with support from a Loyola Provost Fellowship. Before that, I helped study whether expanding Medicaid eligibility in early childhood changed health and mortality decades later, work that was presented at ASHEcon 2026. Alongside my coursework, I'm taking graduate classes in Linear Algebra and Game Theory. For Game Theory, I'm modeling [jury selection as a strategic game](/voir-dire-as-a-game/).

## What I'm doing now

- **LSAT Demon:** Ask Writer and Data Analyst. I explain LSAT questions to students and analyze performance data to improve how practice questions are assigned. (I scored a 174 on the LSAT.)
- **Research:** finishing the volatility-forecasting paper with Professor Kejin Wu, to be presented at Loyola's spring research symposium in 2027.
- **Galactic Dungeons:** a 2D action roguelike I'm building in Unity, mostly for fun.

## Background

- **Finance and insurance:** passed Level I of the CFA Program (2025) and hold a South Carolina Property & Casualty insurance license. I was an analyst for the Rambler Investment Fund, a $1.5M student-run portfolio, and spent a summer as a junior broker at Goosehead Insurance, generating 187 leads and closing 64 deals.
- **Service:** I tutored elementary and middle school students in math at Centro Romero, often in Spanish, and volunteered as an LSAT tutor before joining LSAT Demon's staff.
- **Languages:** English (native), Spanish (B2). In code: Python, R, C#, SAS and Stata.
- **Off the clock:** I played NCAA Division III soccer in 2023–24 and now train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Judo.
