Since September 2026, I've worked at **LSAT Demon** in two roles.

## Ask Writer

When students get stuck on an LSAT question, they can ask for help. I write explanations of why the right answer is right, why the tempting wrong answers are wrong, and how to spot the same pattern next time. I started doing this as a volunteer "Ask Button Hero" in the summer of 2026 before joining the staff.

## Data Analyst

On the data side, I study trends in student performance, subscription retention and the LSAT as a whole. My current project is improving the algorithm that chooses which questions students get during "smart drilling," so each student's practice time goes toward what will help them most.
