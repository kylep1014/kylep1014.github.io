**Parrella, K. & Wu, K. (2026).** *Prediction Inference for Hybrid Models on Realized Volatility Forecasting.* Working paper. [arXiv:2611.13212](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13212)

## The question

Forecasting how volatile the stock market will be is central to risk management and option pricing. Recent work combines classical econometric models with machine learning, and those hybrid models often forecast well. But nearly all of that literature reports a single number, a point estimate, with no sense of how wrong it might be.

A forecast without a range of uncertainty is hard to trust. This paper asks how to put reliable **prediction intervals** around those forecasts.

## What I did

- Developed **bootstrap** and **conformal prediction** methods that wrap prediction intervals around hybrid machine-learning volatility forecasts.
- Wrote all of the code, ran the simulation studies, and analyzed four U.S. stock indices from 2020 to 2025.
- Drafted the manuscript as first author, mentored by Professor Kejin Wu at Loyola University Chicago.
