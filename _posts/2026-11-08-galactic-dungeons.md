Galactic Dungeons is a 2D top-down action roguelike I'm building in Unity, written in C#.

## The premise

An alien species runs an interstellar reality show. They capture contestants from "barbarian" planets and drop them into procedurally generated dungeons for entertainment. The deeper a contestant survives, the more the show escalates, with new mechanics, bosses and better gear. Death ends the run, but not the show: the next contestant steps in.

The tone is game-show spectacle: over-the-top sports-style announcers, a sarcastic AI narrator, and a production crew that is openly manufacturing drama.

## How it plays

- Twin-stick combat: move with the keyboard, aim with the mouse, and mix melee and ranged weapons across two slots.
- A dodge roll with invincibility frames, balanced by timing instead of a cooldown.
- Six floors across three biomes, plus a final boss, built from hand-designed rooms placed procedurally.

## Where it stands

I'm in the first phase: getting the core combat to feel right before building out floors, enemies and story. It's a side project, but it uses the same habits as my research. I write a design document, test small pieces one at a time, and keep the tuning numbers adjustable so the game can be refined through play.
